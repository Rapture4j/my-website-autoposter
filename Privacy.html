Privacy Policy for My Autoposter

Last updated: April 30, 2026

This application ("My Autoposter") automates posting RSS feed content from the owner's website to their own Facebook Page.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data of Facebook users. The app only uses Page Access Tokens provided by the Page admin to publish content they own.

How We Use Data

  • To publish RSS feed items to your Facebook Page (pages_manage_posts)
  • To read Page engagement (pages_read_engagement)

Data Sharing

No data is shared with third parties.
